A Home Far From Home

Tucked in the quiet 4th Ward of Colva, just next to Hotel Amigo Plaza, Aston Ajoy Home Comfort is a family-run homestay welcoming guests since 2018. Your host, Thomas Aston, is a people person who loves lending an ear, and speaks both English and Hindi.

It's a simple, comfortable stay with a private kitchen you can cook in, a trained caretaker on hand, and Colva Beach barely a 3-minute walk away. Whether you're a couple, a family, or a group of friends — you're welcome here.
